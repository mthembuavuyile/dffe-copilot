Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 • Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment (DFFE)

1. Overview & Architecture

DFFE Copilot is an Environmental Intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workspace developed for the Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment (DFFE), based on the Atlas architecture by Vylex Technologies.

DFFE Copilot is engineered as a privacy-centric, stateless RAG platform. Public queries are grounded strictly in published DFFE reports, environmental programmes, and statutory acts without exposing restricted internal departmental records or collecting personal citizen data.

2. Client-Side Key Storage & Credentials

Zero Server Storage for Personal API Keys

When you configure an optional personal OpenRouter API key in DFFE Copilot, it is stored locally in your web browser via localStorage. During inference requests, it is passed transiently via request headers to the stateless backend proxy solely to authenticate your request; it is never persisted into a server database or logged.

3. Workspace Sessions & Chat History

All chat messages, generated report briefs, and investigation histories are stored directly on your local device:

  • Local Persistence: Conversations, bookmarks, and preferences remain in your browser's localStorage (atlas_investigations), with no server-side chat history database.
  • Export Control: You can export full conversations into Markdown or JSON formats with one click.
  • Immediate Deletion: Deleting a session in the DFFE Copilot sidebar immediately removes it from your local browser storage.

4. South African POPIA Compliance & Information Governance

DFFE Copilot operates in alignment with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA, Act 4 of 2013) of the Republic of South Africa:

  • Public vs. Internal Data Separation: The public DFFE RAG corpus contains only verified, published departmental reports and statutory legislation.
  • No Telemetry Selling or Tracking Cookies: DFFE Copilot contains zero third-party advertising trackers or tracking cookies.
  • Right to Erasure: Clearing your browser storage permanently erases all local session history.
