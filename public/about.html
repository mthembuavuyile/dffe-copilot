Launch Workspace

Environmental Intelligence
for South Africa.

DFFE Copilot (Beta) is the conversational Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant and policy workspace for the Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment (DFFE), built on the Atlas architecture by Vylex Technologies. Query published State of the Environment (SoER) reports, EPWP programmes, and NEMA/Climate Change legislation with verified source citations.

Open DFFE Copilot Explore RAG Portal & Chat Bubble

Core Capabilities

Verified DFFE RAG Corpus

Retrieve source-cited answers across South Africa's State of the Environment Reports (SoER), EPWP programmes (Working for Water, Working on Fire, Working for the Coast), and NEMA/Climate Change legislation.

UNEP EnvironmentGPT Filtering

Filter departmental knowledge by Report Series, Publication Year, and Target Audience (General Public, DFFE Officials, Policy Analysts, and EIA Practitioners).

PDF & CSV Document Intelligence

Attach 50-page environmental PDFs or SAAQIS/SAWIC CSV datasets for instant client-side text extraction, statistical summaries, and OCR scanning.

Scientific & GHG Modelling

Native KaTeX and mhchem rendering for greenhouse gas inventories ($\ce{CO2}$-eq), atmospheric dispersion models, and water catchment calculations.

5-Tab Artifacts Canvas

Inspect full DFFE report briefs, ministerial summaries, interactive dashboards, and code diffs side by side in a dedicated split-screen workspace.

Multi-Model Resilience

Switch seamlessly between 7 curated reasoning engines (up to 1M context tokens) with automatic rate-limit failover and optional BYOK support.

8 Investigation Modes

Tailor your session with Auto, Research, Solve, Build, Engineer, Experiment, Reason, and Discover modes plus the DFFE Environmental Specialist preset.

26 Built-In Tools + DFFE RAG

Combines verified DFFE knowledge retrieval with live weather, OpenStreetMap + Leaflet mapping, web search, unit conversion, symbolic math, and OCR tools.

Built for DFFE & Public Environmental Discovery.

DFFE Copilot is engineered by the DFFE IT Business Solutions Software Development team to help citizens, researchers, and departmental officials retrieve authoritative environmental evidence without manually reading 50-page PDF reports.

Built on the open-source Atlas workspace architecture by Vylex Technologies, it delivers enterprise RAG capabilities in-house without multi-million-rand vendor lock-in.

  • Synthesizing State of the Environment (SoER) findings with official citations
  • Navigating EPWP programmes (Working for Water, Working on Fire, Working for the Coast)
  • Checking NEMA Section 24 EIA Listing Notices and National Screening Tool requirements
  • Analyzing SAAQIS air quality and SAWIC waste datasets in the Artifacts Canvas
